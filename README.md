# Elemental Hearts

Elemental Hearts adds a large collection of permanent upgrade hearts to Terraria. Each heart is themed after a material, enemy, boss, potion, biome, or supported cross-mod source, giving health progression more variety throughout a playthrough.

## Features

- **Permanent heart upgrades:** Consume hearts to permanently increase your maximum life.
- **Many material hearts:** Craft hearts from blocks, woods, ores, gems, herbs, fruits, biome materials, souls, and late-game resources.
- **Tiered progression:** Hearts are grouped into Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Exotic, and Mythic tiers.
- **Configurable health gains:** Server settings can tune how much life each tier grants.
- **Boss hearts:** Bosses can drop special Exotic hearts, with extra reward pressure around first kills.
- **Active ability hearts:** Some special hearts unlock toggleable powers instead of granting health.
- **Life Shards:** Consumed hearts feed into a shard economy used by active abilities and related progression.
- **Potion hearts:** Potion-themed hearts turn familiar utility effects into permanent upgrades.
- **Pacified hearts:** A themed heart line tied to pacification-style progression.
- **Vital Canopy content:** Adds a life-themed biome experience with custom ambience, music, tiles, plants, chests, and related rewards.
- **Animate bosses:** Adds tiered Animate boss encounters with custom visuals, sounds, projectiles, and music.
- **Heart checklist UI:** Track discovered, locked, unlocked, active, passive, and milestone hearts from an in-game interface.
- **Custom visuals and audio:** Hearts use tiered glow, consume effects, sounds, particles, and curated color palettes.
- **Multiplayer support:** Heart consumption and related progression are built for multiplayer worlds.
- **Cross-mod hearts:** Includes heart content for supported mods such as Calamity, Thorium, and Consolaria when those mods are present.

## Supported Content Themes

- Vanilla terrain and building materials
- Wood and biome variants
- Gems, ores, and hardmode materials
- Herbs, fruits, mushrooms, and potion effects
- Boss and event rewards
- Life Shards and Vital Canopy rewards
- Calamity materials and bosses
- Thorium materials and bosses
- Consolaria materials and bosses

## Configuration

Elemental Hearts includes server-side settings for heart health values, challenge-style progression, shard generation, and active ability costs.
