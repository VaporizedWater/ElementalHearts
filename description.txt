Elemental Hearts adds permanent stat-boosters. Find your materials, find your bench, get more power! (Or fight a boss and harvest their organs!)


Also includes 5 polished boss fights, a new mini-structure, fully integrated checklist, an economy, and many other new features.


Designed to fit naturally into every modpack, and make your experience more fun and rewarding!


If you're worried it might be too OP, try it out! From unlocking ground pounds to expanding your inventory slots, I promise it's the best thing since sliced bread and won't change the game's intended difficulty!


Have fun completing your collection!
